## TBA 2.0.5: Core Curriculum returns

**Core Curriculum 0.2.0** is back, ported to NeoForge.

- **Titles** show in front of your name in chat and the tab list. Pick yours with `/titles` or `/title set <title>`.
  Every player can change their own title now, not just ops.
- **`/submit <build name>`** sends screenshots of a build to slashAI for review; `/submissions` lists yours.
- **`/nominate`** recognizes someone who helped you; `/recognitions` shows the nominations you've received.
- **`/link`** links your Discord account.
- **The welcome screen** opens when you join, or press **V** any time.
- Server stats sync to the website again.
- Players still on an older pack can join; Core Curriculum's screens just ask them to update.

180 mods, Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.265.

**Downloads:** CurseForge App: `TBA-2.0.5.zip` (Windows), `-linux`, `-macos-arm64`, `-macos-x86_64` variants. Prism
Launcher: `TBA-2.0.5.mrpack` and the same platform variants. Pick the one for your computer: StreamCraft ships
per-platform files.
